Q3 and Q4 · An operational list that is a record, and a proof that is evidence

Q3 · Offer expiry watchlist

The offer is read by version, because the row is overwritten in place. Acceptance is an anti-join at the data cut-off. Expiry-pending offers lead the list, since a customer can still accept until the expiry job runs. Each morning’s list is snapshotted: what ops was told is a record.

Q4 · Proof that PII was masked

Six evidence tables, none of them business data, exported daily to a write-once bucket in a security-owned account. A probe principal reads masked columns every day and tries the forbidden layers. Break-glass reads join to their ticket.
