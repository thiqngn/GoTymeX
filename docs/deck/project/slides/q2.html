Q2 · The platform proves what the sources held, and says what it cannot prove

Each input read as of its own time from the history tables, with an evidence grade: engine copy, source as of, or not captured.

Ambiguity is a flag, not a guess. Two bureau fetches in the read window flag the bureau input. A late input opens a new record version; the old one stays.

Re-derived weekly against a stored hash. A mismatch is published as a defect, never corrected. Legacy decisions are served as they are, graded NOT_CAPTURED.

Say the boundary out loud; it is the strongest thing in Q2: the platform proves what the sources held and what the engine copied onto its decision row. It cannot prove what the engine actually read unless the engine says so. That is where the confirm item comes from: ask the decision service for an inputs snapshot event keyed on application number and decision version. The decision row already copies the employer fields, so the rest is a small step, and it lifts every answer to the top evidence grade. Declared income has two homes and which one the engine used is open; the record carries both with their read times.
