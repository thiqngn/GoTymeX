Gold is a plain star, and beside it, records of what someone was shown

Three facts, five dimensions, no stored aggregates. Every number is a join and a group by. Metrics are code-owned views, so Product owns a definition by owning a file.

Keys are the business codes. Channel, branch, product, the bank-local date. A join reads the way a business user says it.

Written once, never edited: the frozen cohort, the decision input record, the morning watchlist, the gate verdict. A frozen row stores names as text, so it never depends on a dimension that changes later.

The facts: one row per submitted application, one per disbursement with a negative row per reversal, one per whitelist entry, for whitelist-to-application conversion. The dimensions include a versioned drop-off bucket mapping, so the roll-up from fine outcome codes to the brief’s five reasons is a seed Product can change with a diff, not logic. The written-once records are the part to stress: what a regulator was shown, what compliance was shown, what ops was told each morning, and whether a cohort was allowed to publish, each a row that cannot be edited afterwards.
