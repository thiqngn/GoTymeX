Four questions, and what each one really tests

QThe brief asksWhat it tests
Q1Share of a month’s submissions funded within 14 days, by channel, and why the rest did not fundA cohort that never moves, a reason that always sums
Q2Reconstruct the inputs the credit engine saw at decision time, even if the sources have since changedHistory that is captured, never reconstructed
Q3Each morning: offers expiring in 24 hours and unaccepted, plus offers expired without action in 7 daysAn operational list with a record of what ops was told
Q4Prove to a regulator that PII was masked before any analyst touched the funnel data for a quarterEvidence, not configuration

Three words carry the whole design: append-only capture, as-of reads, and an honest answer that says what was not captured.

3

Read the right-hand column, not the left. Q1 tests whether a cohort can be pinned so a rewrite cannot move it, and whether the drop-off reasons sum to the cohort. Q2 is only answerable with append-only capture: the phrase “even if the sources have since changed” rules out reading current rows. Q3 is a morning SLA, not analytics, and the list itself becomes a record. Q4 asks for proof after the fact, so the answer is evidence tables, not a description of masks.
